Twelve minutes without a breath

Published 2:25 pm Tuesday, November 10, 2026

R. A Mathews

Take a deep breath and hold it. Many people can last 20 seconds. Some may be able to reach a minute.

What about 12 minutes? Impossible!

And yet author James Nestor says in his international bestseller “Breath” that a trained diver can do just that—hold the breath for 12 minutes. It seems like a miracle, but controlled breathing dates back thousands of years.

Recent research on the vagus nerve, stretching from the brain to the abdomen, has revived interest in breath work. And Nestor says it doesn’t take much effort. He cites a study: “Just five minutes of breathing exercises per day could lower blood pressure as much as exercise or drugs.” He says, “Consciously controlling breathing can significantly influence … sleep quality, heart rate, and blood flow.”

Just the breath? It’s so unexpected!

The miracles of Jesus also have a foundation dating back thousands of years, but a completely different one. Let me show you.

Scripture has a heart-wrenching story about a desperate father who brought his only son to Jesus. The boy suffered from seizures that threw him to the ground and sometimes into fire and into water. The disciples had tried to help him and failed.

Filled with anguish, the father approached Jesus and said, “…if You can do anything, take pity on us and help us!” (Mark 9:22, NASB)

Many lose sight of what the father actually said. Look again. “…if You can do anything, take pity on us and help us!”

The Lord heard the words and repeated them, “If You can?” (Mark 9:23, NASB)

And then the Lord told the man: “All things are possible for the one who believes.” (Mark 9:23, NASB)

The father’s reaction is truly painful. Fearing that his faith wasn’t good enough to save his son, the father cried out: “I do believe; help my unbelief!” (Mark 9:24, NASB)

And that’s good enough for Jesus. The Lord rebuked the spirit and healed the boy.

Pay attention to what happens next. After the miracle, the disciples wanted to know why they had failed to heal the boy.

“And [Jesus] said to them, ‘This kind cannot come out by anything except prayer.’” (Mark 9:29, NASB)

Prayer is powerful. Stay close to God in prayer with all you have. It takes determination to ask Him day after day for what you desperately need and cannot see happening. But know that God is a God of great miracles.

Twelve minutes without a breath is now grounded in scientific studies. The miracles of Jesus are grounded in faith and prayer.

“Call unto me, and I will answer thee, and shew thee great and mighty things, which thou knowest not.” (Jeremiah 33:3, KJV)

The Lord will show you great and mighty things if you stay close to Him. Pray without ceasing for what you need.
