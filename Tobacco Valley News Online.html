Cemetery Tours 2026

Eureka Community Players hosts an annual tour at the Tobacco Valley Cemetery, using actors to honor the memories of a few of the people buried there by telling their stories. Sunny weather for the 2026 version held on Sept. 20 was a welcome contrast to the icy rain of the previous year. Sharon LaBonty of ECP estimated that about 100 people attended.
