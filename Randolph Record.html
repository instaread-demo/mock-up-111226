Prep soccer: Season ends for county’s teams in third round

The season ended for Randolph County’s remaining boys’ soccer teams in the state playoffs Monday night.

Three teams reached the third round, but none of them advanced beyond that.

This means that Southwestern Randolph’s reign has ended after winning a state championship for the first time last year.

Asheboro and Providence Grove also exited after reaching the second week of the postseason.

 

Class 6A

Asheboro reached the third round of the West Region with a pair of narrow victories, but the Blue Comets couldn’t handle top-seeded Charlotte Catholic.

Ninth-seeded Asheboro’s 6-0 loss marked the end of an 18-8-1 season.

Last week, the ninth-seeded Blue Comets outlasted No. 24 seed Olympic (5-16-5) through a scoreless regulation and overtime, advancing via penalty kicks in the first round.

Next, Asheboro won 2-1 at eighth-seeded Northern Guilford (15-5) in a clash between Triad Area Athletic Conference co-champions. Brian Bautisa and Sebastian Silva had goals for the Blue Comets, with Jonathan Ortega and Juan Macias picking up assists.

 

Class 4A

Defending state champion Southwestern Randolph was bounced with a 3-1 loss to second-seeded North Pitt in the East Region

The Cougars, after reaching the state finals in consecutive seasons, finished with a 13-9-1 record.

Seventh-seeded Southwestern Randolph, following a bye, won last week’s second-round game by 7-2 against No. 10 seed Eastern Wayne (11-6-1).

** Randleman (9-13-4) made it to the second round before falling in the East Region.

The No. 16 seed Tigers scored two second-half goals to edge No. 17 seed Eden Morehead 2-0 in the first round.

Randleman’s season ended later last week with a 7-0 loss at top-seeded Clinton.

** Uwharrie Charter Academy (2-14-2), with the No. 24 seed, lost 9-0 at ninth-seeded Graham in the East Region’s first round.

 

Class 3A

Providence Grove made it to the third round of the East Region before this week’s 5-0 setback at fourth-seeded Wake Prep.

The No. 12 seed Patriots (12-6-3) suffered their third shutout of the season.

In the first round, the Patriots won 4-1 at home against No. 12 seed Princeton (7-15-1), powered by Saul Cortes-Aguirre’s two goals and one assist along with one goal each by Kevin Bautista-Jimenez and Criss Cancino-Lopez.

Providence Grove then prevailed in a penalty-kick shootout after a scoreless regulation and overtime at fifth-seeded McMichael (16-5) last Thursday.  The Patriots had a 4-2 edge in the shootout.

** Eastern Randolph was finished after a 4-2 first-round loss at No. 10 seed West Bladen. The Wildcats completed a 5-13-1 season.

** In the West Region, Trinity had a first-round bye as the No. 8 seed and then lost 2-1 to ninth-seeded West Lincoln.

The Bulldogs posted a 12-9 record.

** No. 20 seed Wheatmore was bounced 7-0 at No. 13 seed Hendersonville in the first round. The Warriors went 4-15-3 this season.
