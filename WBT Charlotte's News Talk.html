Election Fallout, Hegseth Impeachment Push and America's Political Future

Brett kicks off the program by talking about efforts to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth and the growing political tensions surrounding Congressman Thomas Massie and other lawmakers challenging the Trump administration. Brett questions the motivations behind the impeachment push, arguing that lawmakers should be focused on national security and addressing the country’s most pressing concerns rather than pursuing political battles.

Beth Troutman from Good Morning BT is also here for this Tuesday’s episode of Crossing the Streams. Brett and Beth talk about the aftermath of the recent election, the transition leading up to the new administration, and some of the surprising results in North Carolina congressional races. They discuss the defeats of Richard Hudson and Tim Moore, the ongoing vote counts in other races, and what the election could mean for voters and the country over the next two years.Beth also shares what she and Good Morning BT have coming up, including a conversation with cybersecurity expert Teresa Payton
