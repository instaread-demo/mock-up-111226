5 Festive Aldi Finds To Add To Your Cart This Week: Melting Hot Cocoa Bombs & More

Aldi is getting into the holiday spirit with this week’s new Aldi Finds, and there are plenty of fun seasonal items to look forward to. From festive Disney pajamas and Thanksgiving-themed dog toys to cozy home accessories and sweet holiday treats, the upcoming selection has something for just about everyone. Whether you’re getting a head start on holiday shopping or simply looking for a few fun additions to your next grocery run, these finds are worth checking out.

Plus, all five picks are under $30, making them affordable options for gifting, decorating, or treating yourself. Here are five Aldi Finds to keep an eye out for next week!

1. Holiday Minnie & Mickey PJ Pants - $9.99

Disney fans will want to keep an eye out for these Holiday Minnie & Mickey PJs. At just $9.99, these festive pants are a fun way to get into the holiday spirit without spending a fortune. If you're a matching holiday pajama family, consider this a fantastic option.

The red plaid design brings a classic Christmas feel, while the Disney characters make them especially cute for fans of Mickey and Minnie Mouse. If you can't afford a Disney vacation this winter, you can at least cozy up in these!

aldi

2. Thanksgiving Dog Toys - $4.99

Don't forget about your four-legged family members when Thanksgiving rolls around! Aldi is bringing some Thanksgiving fun to its pet section with these Heart to Tail Thanksgiving Dog Toys.

Choose from four seriously fun shapes: corn, pumpkin pie, turkey leg, and roast turkey. That way your furry friend can still enjoy Thanksgiving dinner—and for just $4.99! This is a fun, affordable addition to the family-centered celebration we know and love. Because Fido deserves a place at the table, too.

aldi

3. Biddeford Emerald Heated Throw - $25.99

The days are shorter, the nights are longer, and the air is chillier. It's time to get cozy and warm with a heated blanket, and Aldi's here to help.

This Biddeford Emerald Heated Throw measures 50 by 62 inches and comes in a rich emerald green shade that's perfect for the season. Whether you're curling up on the couch for a movie night or looking for an extra layer of warmth while relaxing at home, this throw is a practical seasonal purchase. At $25.99, it's also considerably less expensive than many heated blankets you'll find elsewhere.

aldi

4. Racine Danish Kringles - $6.99

All I can say is yum. Aldi is also bringing back a sweet bakery find with its Racine Danish Kringles. The 14-ounce pastry is priced at $6.99 and makes a tempting option for breakfast, dessert, or serving alongside coffee when guests come over. It's available in two flavors: raspberry and almond. Why not grab both?

With these tasty fillings and flaky pastry, this seasonal treat brings joy to ordinary winter mornings—or makes festive ones even more cheerful. If you're planning any holiday gatherings, it's worth picking one up to share.

aldi

5. Choceur Melting Cocoa Bomb - $2.69

Finally, Aldi has another adorable holiday treat coming to shelves: the Choceur Melting Cocoa Bomb. This chocolate cocoa bomb is a festive way to enjoy a cozy cup of hot chocolate as temperatures drop. Just add it to a mug of warm milk and watch it melt into a sweet seasonal drink.

But the best part is the cute shape. There's a penguin option, which features classic milk chocolate, and a snowman, which features white chocolate, marshmallows, and dark chocolate drops. My mouth is watering! At only $2.69, it's an affordable little treat to pick up for yourself or give as a stocking stuffer.
